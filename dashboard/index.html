How the score works

Transparent points — a claim can trigger several rules (max 100)

R1 · High frequency
> 3 claims in 12 months → 30 pts
R2 · Unusual amount
> 3× category average → 30 pts
R3 · Rapid succession
another claim within 30 days → 20 pts
R4 · Repeated vehicle
same vehicle on >1 claim → 20 pts
Limitations: false positives are expected (a bad year ≠ fraud); thresholds are policy choices to be tuned on real outcomes; small samples carry weak signal; and because this data is synthetic, the screening partly rediscovers its own generator. Always confirm with a human review.
